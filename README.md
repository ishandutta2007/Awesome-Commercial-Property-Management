# Awesome-Commercial-Property-Management

## Top Commercial Property Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on CRE Portfolio Management, Lease Administration, Tenant Operations, Accounting, Facilities & IWMS*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Commercial Property Management**. These systems support commercial real estate (office, retail, industrial, mixed-use) and related portfolios with leasing, accounting, tenant services, work orders, and facilities management.



**Examples** include Yardi Voyager, MRI Software, AppFolio Property Manager, Buildium, DoorLoop, Propertyware, Rent Manager, Entrata, ResMan, TenantCloud, Building Engines, Visual Lease, RealPage, Trimble Manhattan, Spacewell, FM:Systems, Archibus, and IBM TRIRIGA (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full enterprise commercial property management and IWMS platforms remain largely commercial. Open options include **Condo**, **OpenProperty**, **Odoo PMS modules**, and related self-hosted property/facility tools. This section expands those while remaining realistic about the commercial gap for large CRE portfolios.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Yardi Voyager](https://www.yardi.com/)**  

  Enterprise real estate platform widely used for complex mixed-asset commercial and residential portfolios—accounting, leasing, and operations.



- **[MRI Software](https://www.mrisoftware.com/)**  

  Configurable commercial property management and investment suite strong on multi-entity accounting and enterprise workflows.



- **[AppFolio Property Manager](https://www.appfolio.com/)**  

  Modern property management platform popular for mid-to-large operators, with strong automation and mixed-portfolio capabilities.



- **[Buildium](https://www.buildium.com/)**  

  Property management software for small to mid-size portfolios covering accounting, leasing, and resident/tenant tools.



- **[DoorLoop](https://www.doorloop.com/)**  

  All-in-one property management platform used for residential and some commercial/retail office portfolios.



- **[Propertyware](https://www.propertyware.com/)**  

  Property management software focused on operations, accounting, and portfolio administration (RealPage family in some contexts).



- **[Rent Manager](https://www.rentmanager.com/)**  

  Highly configurable property management system strong on accounting and operational flexibility for mixed portfolios.



- **[Entrata](https://www.entrata.com/)**  

  Property management and resident experience platform with modules used across multifamily and related commercial operations.



- **[ResMan](https://www.myresman.com/)**  

  Property management software for multifamily and related portfolios with accounting and operational tools.



- **[TenantCloud](https://www.tenantcloud.com/)**  

  Cloud property management platform aimed at landlords and smaller managers for leasing, rent, and maintenance.



- **[Building Engines](https://www.buildingengines.com/)**  

  Commercial building operations and facilities management platform for work orders, inspections, and tenant services.



- **[Visual Lease](https://www.visuallease.com/)**  

  Lease accounting and administration platform focused on commercial lease portfolios and compliance (ASC 842 / IFRS 16).



- **[RealPage](https://www.realpage.com/)**  

  Broad real estate technology suite with commercial and multifamily property management, accounting, and operational modules.



- **[Trimble Manhattan](https://www.trimble.com/)**  

  Integrated workplace and real estate management solutions for corporate and commercial portfolios.



- **[Spacewell](https://www.spacewell.com/)**  

  Smart building and workplace management platform for commercial facilities and space optimization.



- **[FM:Systems](https://fmsystems.com/)**  

  Integrated workplace management system (IWMS) for space, facilities, and real estate operations.



- **[Archibus](https://archibus.com/)**  

  Long-standing IWMS and facilities management platform for space, maintenance, and real estate portfolios.



- **[IBM TRIRIGA](https://www.ibm.com/products/tririga)**  

  Enterprise IWMS for real estate, facilities, and environmental performance management at large scale.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Condo](https://github.com/open-condo-software/condo)**  

  Open-source property management platform for tickets, resident contacts, properties, payments, invoices, and a service marketplace—suited to property management companies and shared properties.



- **[OpenProperty](https://github.com/clawnify/OpenProperty)**  

  Open-source, self-hosted property management software positioned as an alternative to TenantCloud, AppFolio, Buildium, and Propertyware—units, tenants, leases, rent, and work orders.



- **[Odoo PMS (OCA)](https://github.com/OCA/pms)**  

  Open-source Property Management System modules on Odoo for multi-property management and related real estate workflows (AGPL).



- **[Microrealestate](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source long-term rental / property management oriented projects for self-hosted portfolio tracking.



- **[Odoo real-estate and facility modules](https://github.com/odoo)**  

  Community and OCA modules that can be extended for commercial leasing, maintenance, and asset tracking.



- **[Open CAFM / facilities open stacks](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects combining CMMS, space, and asset tracking that can support commercial building operations.



- **[Lease abstraction and accounting open helpers](https://github.com/)**  

  Scripts and templates for lease data management and reporting (complementary to commercial lease admin tools).



- **[PostgreSQL + GIS open property inventories](https://www.postgresql.org/)**  

  Open database and mapping foundations used to build custom commercial portfolio and space registers.



- **[ERP frameworks (ERPNext / Odoo) for custom CRE](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  General open ERP platforms frequently customized for property accounting, vendors, and work orders.



- **[Documentation and self-hosted PMS playbooks](https://github.com/open-condo-software/condo)**  

  Guides for deploying open property management stacks and integrating maintenance or payment flows.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **Condo** or **OpenProperty** for tickets, contacts, leases, and rent tracking in smaller or mid-size operations.

- Extending **Odoo PMS / ERPNext** when full accounting and customization are required.

- Combining open CMMS and GIS tools for facilities-heavy commercial buildings.

- Accepting that enterprise CRE accounting, multi-entity ledgers, complex lease administration (CAM, recoveries), lender reporting, and IWMS depth still favor commercial platforms (Yardi, MRI, RealPage, TRIRIGA, Archibus, FM:Systems, Visual Lease, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, cost control, and integration flexibility.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Model properties and leases in Odoo/Condo/OpenProperty → track work orders and tenants → report via open BI tools → optionally layer GIS for space. Suitable for smaller portfolios, cooperatives, and technical operators. Large commercial and institutional portfolios almost always run on commercial PMS/IWMS platforms.
